🎓 Biography

Hi👋, I'm Quanjian Song (宋泉鉴), a Master candidate in the Department of Computer Science and Technology at Xiamen University.
I am currently researching in the Media Analytics and Computing (MAC) Lab, supervised by Prof. Rongrong Ji and Prof. Liujuan Cao.
My current research interests primarily include:

  • Visual Generation for Application: Controllable Video Generation, Streaming/Interactive Avatar/World Models.
  • Visual Generation for Post-Training: AR Distillation, Few-Step Distillation, Reinforcement Learning.
Below is my personal experience:
  • 2026/06 - Now: Streaming & Interactive World Model · DouYin AIGC Group Project Intern · ByteDance.
  • 2026/01 - 2026/06: Streaming & Interactive Video Customization · Taobao & Tmall Group Research Intern · Alibaba.
  • 2025/08 - 2025/12: Video-to-Video Perspective Translation · Multimodal Generation Research Intern · First Intelligence.
I am actively seeking opportunities for the 2027 graduate recruitment cycle. If you are interested in my research or potential collaboration, please feel free to reach out!
